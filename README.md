# 😤 Excuse-Meister
### *The Petty Excuse Generator — Master of Ridiculous Reasons*

> "Life's too short for boring excuses."

---

## What even is this?

You know that moment when you're late to class, forgot your homework, bailed on plans, or ghosted a meeting — and your brain goes completely blank on reasons?

**Excuse-Meister fixes that.**

It generates **funny, witty, and suspiciously believable excuses** tailored to your exact situation. Whether you're dodging a 9am meeting or explaining why you haven't texted back in 4 days — we've got you covered. Beautifully. Pettily. Ridiculously.

---

## ✨ Features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🗂️ **3 Categories** | Work, School, Social — because excuses are situational |
| 🎯 **Pre-built Scenarios** | 15 specific situations (e.g. "Forgot homework", "Skipping a meeting") |
| ⚡ **Excuse Generator** | Randomised, scenario-tailored output — no two sessions alike |
| 💪 **Excuse Strength Meter** | Rates your excuse: 😇 Believable → 😏 Petty → 🤡 Ridiculous |
| 📋 **Copy to Clipboard** | One tap and send — paste directly into your texts |
| 👍 **Upvote System** | Vote for the best excuses |
| 🏆 **Pettiest Excuses Leaderboard** | Top 5 most-upvoted excuses, ranked with 🥇🥈🥉 medals |
| 💾 **Persistent Votes** | Leaderboard saved to `localStorage` — survives page reloads |

## 🚀 Running It

**Option 1 — Just double-click it:**
```
index.html  ← open in any browser
```
No server. No install. No npm. No suffering.

**Option 2 — Serve locally (for clipboard support):**
```bash
npx serve .
# then open http://localhost:3000
```

> ⚠️ The Copy to Clipboard button requires `localhost` or `https`. On a plain `file://` URL it may silently fail in some browsers. Everything else works fine.
